**IELTS Speaking Part 2** da sizga mavzu yozilgan kartochka (cue card) beriladi: **1 daqiqa** tayyorlanasiz va **2 daqiqagacha** to'xtovsiz gapirasiz. Yuqori ball uchun eng ishonchli usul — kartochkadagi savollar tartibida, har biriga detal qo'shib, aniq boshlanish va yakun bilan gapirish. Quyida tayyor tuzilma, uchta to'liq namunaviy javob (o'zimiz yozgan) va mashq rejasi bor.

Speaking'ning umumiy tuzilishi va baholash mezonlari haqida: [IELTS Speaking: uch qism va maslahatlar](/blog/ielts-speaking-qismlar-va-maslahatlar).

## Cue card qanday ko'rinadi

Kartochkada bitta asosiy topshiriq va 3–4 ta yo'naltiruvchi savol bo'ladi:

> **Describe a skill you learned that you think is useful.**
> You should say:
> - what the skill is
> - when and how you learned it
> - how often you use it
> - and explain why you think it is useful.

Kartochkalar odatda besh guruhdan biriga tegishli: **odam**, **joy**, **narsa (buyum)**, **voqea (tajriba)** va **faoliyat (odat, ko'nikma)**. Shuning uchun har bir guruh uchun bitta-ikkita "universal" hikoya tayyorlab qo'ysangiz, deyarli har qanday mavzuga moslashtira olasiz.

## 1 daqiqada qanday tayyorlanish kerak

Qog'ozga to'liq gap yozmang — vaqt yetmaydi va keyin qog'ozdan o'qib qolasiz. Har bir yo'naltiruvchi savolga 2–3 ta kalit so'z yozing:

| Savol | Eslatma (kalit so'zlar) |
|---|---|
| what | touch typing — 10 fingers |
| when/how | 2nd year uni, free online course, 20 min/day |
| how often | every day — essays, work emails |
| why useful | saves time, fewer mistakes, IELTS computer test |

Oxirgi "explain why" bandiga eng ko'p kalit so'z yozing — u eng uzun va eng "ball beradigan" qism.

## Universal javob tuzilmasi

2 daqiqalik nutqni beshta qismga bo'ling:

1. **Kirish (10 soniya).** Mavzuni bir gap bilan nomlang: *I'd like to talk about...*
2. **Kontekst (20–25 soniya).** Kim, qachon, qayerda — asosiy faktlar.
3. **Rivojlanish (40–50 soniya).** Eng qiziq detallar: nima bo'ldi, nimani ko'rdingiz, nimani his qildingiz. O'tgan zamonlarni aralashtiring.
4. **Sabab yoki ahamiyat (30–40 soniya).** "Explain why" savoliga chuqur javob — bu yerda murakkab gaplar va fikr bildirish.
5. **Yakun (5–10 soniya).** Qisqa xulosa yoki kelajakka bog'lash: *So that's why... I'm planning to...*

Bu tuzilma Fluency and Coherence mezoniga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri yordam beradi: imtihonchi nutqingizda aniq ketma-ketlikni ko'radi.

## Namunaviy javob 1: foydali ko'nikma

**Cue card:** *Describe a skill you learned that you think is useful.* (yuqoridagi kartochka)

> I'd like to talk about touch typing, which basically means typing with all ten fingers without looking at the keyboard. It sounds like a small thing, but it has made a surprisingly big difference in my life.
>
> I picked it up during my second year at university. At that time I was writing a lot of assignments, and I noticed that I was spending ages hunting for letters with two fingers. A friend of mine recommended a free online course, so I set myself a rule: twenty minutes of practice every evening, no matter how tired I was. The first two weeks were honestly frustrating, because I was actually slower than before. But after about a month, something clicked, and my speed roughly doubled.
>
> These days I use it constantly — for essays, for work emails, even for chatting with friends. I'd say there isn't a single day when I don't rely on it.
>
> As for why it's useful, I think there are two main reasons. First, it saves an enormous amount of time; tasks that used to take me an hour now take maybe forty minutes. Second, and this is something I didn't expect, it helps me think more clearly. When I don't have to focus on the keyboard, I can concentrate on what I'm actually trying to say. It's also going to be handy for my IELTS exam, since I've chosen the computer-delivered version. So all in all, it's one of the best investments of twenty minutes a day I've ever made.

**Nima uchun bu javob kuchli:**

- aniq kirish va yakun bor;
- zamonlarning turli shakllari: *picked up* (past simple), *was writing* (past continuous), *has made* (present perfect), *used to take*;
- idiomatik iboralar: *spending ages, something clicked, all in all*;
- "why" qismida ikkita sabab va kutilmagan detal (*helps me think more clearly*).

## Namunaviy javob 2: odam

**Cue card:**

> **Describe a person who has influenced your choice of career or studies.**
> You should say:
> - who this person is
> - how you know them
> - what they did that influenced you
> - and explain how your life might be different without them.

> The person I'm going to describe is my former chemistry teacher, Dilnoza opa, who taught me in the last two years of school.
>
> I first met her when I was about fifteen. To be honest, at that point I wasn't interested in science at all; I was planning to study economics, mainly because my older cousin had done the same. Her lessons, though, were completely different from anything I'd experienced before. Instead of making us memorise formulas, she'd start each topic with a real-life question, like why bread goes stale or how a phone battery stores energy, and then we'd work backwards to the theory.
>
> What really influenced me, though, was a small project she suggested. She noticed that I was asking a lot of questions after class, so she encouraged me to test the water quality in our neighbourhood for a regional science competition. I didn't win, but spending weeks collecting samples and analysing the results made me realise that this was the kind of work I genuinely enjoyed.
>
> If I hadn't met her, I think I would probably be studying something I'm not passionate about, simply because it seemed like the safe option. Thanks to her, I'm now applying for an environmental engineering programme, and whenever I get stuck, I still try to approach the problem the way she taught us — by starting with a real question.

**E'tibor bering:** shart gap (*If I hadn't met her, I would probably be...*) "explain how your life might be different" savoliga tabiiy javob bo'ldi va Grammatical Range'ni ko'rsatdi.

## Namunaviy javob 3: joy

**Cue card:**

> **Describe a place in your city where you like to spend time.**
> You should say:
> - where it is
> - what it looks like
> - what you do there
> - and explain why you like spending time there.

> I'd like to describe a small café-library near the old part of my city. It's tucked away in a quiet side street, about a ten-minute walk from the main bazaar, so most tourists walk straight past it without noticing.
>
> From the outside it looks fairly ordinary, but inside it's really cosy. There are tall wooden shelves full of second-hand books in Uzbek, Russian and English, and you're allowed to read anything while you have your tea. The lighting is warm, there's usually some quiet music in the background, and in winter they keep a small heater in the corner, which makes it feel like someone's living room rather than a business.
>
> I mostly go there on weekend mornings. I usually order a pot of green tea, find a seat by the window and either read or work on my English. Recently, I've been using it as my IELTS study spot: I do one reading passage, then reward myself with a novel for half an hour.
>
> The main reason I like it is the atmosphere. At home there are always distractions, whereas there it's calm, but not silent, which I find perfect for concentrating. On top of that, the owners organise a small book-exchange evening once a month, and I've met several interesting people that way. So for me it's not just a café; it's a place where I can both switch off and feel productive at the same time.

## Tez-tez uchraydigan mavzular guruhlari

Quyidagi mavzular turli shakllarda qayta-qayta keladi. Har bir guruh uchun bitta hikoya tayyorlang:

| Guruh | Namunaviy mavzular |
|---|---|
| Odam | do'st, o'qituvchi, qo'shni, mashhur odam, oila a'zosi, tadbirkor |
| Joy | shahardagi sevimli joy, sayohat qilgan joy, tinch joy, park, bino |
| Buyum | sovg'a, kitob, foydali qurilma, kiyim, sotib olgan narsa |
| Voqea | bayram, muvaffaqiyat, kechikkan holat, kutilmagan yangilik, to'y |
| Faoliyat | ko'nikma, sport, sevimli mashg'ulot, o'rganmoqchi bo'lgan narsa |

**Maslahat:** bitta hikoya bir nechta mavzuga mos keladi. Yuqoridagi café-library hikoyasini *a quiet place*, *a place where you study*, *a building you like* yoki *a place you recommend to tourists* mavzulariga ham moslashtirsa bo'ladi.

## Part 2 dan keyin: Part 3 ga ko'prik

Part 2 tugagach, imtihonchi ba'zan bir-ikki qisqa savol beradi, keyin esa shu mavzu asosidagi Part 3 muhokamasiga o'tadi. Masalan, foydali ko'nikma haqida gapirgan bo'lsangiz:

- *Which skills do young people need most nowadays?*
- *Should schools teach practical skills or academic subjects?*

Part 3 da shaxsiy hikoya emas, **umumiy fikr** kutiladi: fikr → sabab → misol → qarama-qarshi tomon.

## Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

- **1 daqiqani to'liq gap yozishga sarflash.** Natijada qog'ozdan o'qiysiz va ravonlik tushadi.
- **Faqat hozirgi zamon.** Hikoya o'tgan zamonda bo'lsa, *was doing, had done, used to* shakllarini ishlating.
- **"Explain why" qismini bir gap bilan tugatish.** Bu eng muhim qism — kamida 30 soniya.
- **Mavzudan chiqib ketish.** *A place you like* o'rniga butun shahar tarixini aytib berish.
- **Juda qisqa javob.** 1 daqiqadan kam gapirish — ko'nikmalaringizni ko'rsatishga imkon bermaydi.

## Mashq rejasi (2 hafta)

1. Har kuni bitta kartochka: taymer bilan 1 daqiqa tayyorlanish, 2 daqiqa gapirish, telefonda yozib olish.
2. Yozuvni tinglab, uchta narsani belgilang: uzun pauzalar, takrorlangan so'zlar, grammatik xatolar.
3. Ertasi kuni xuddi shu kartochkani qayta gapiring.
4. Haftada bir marta beshta guruhdan biriga yangi universal hikoya tayyorlang.

**Matching Headings** savolida har bir paragrafga sarlavhalar ro'yxatidan mos sarlavhani tanlaysiz. Asosiy qoida: sarlavha paragrafning **bitta detaliga emas, butun asosiy g'oyasiga** mos kelishi kerak. Eng ishonchli usul — avval paragrafning asosiy g'oyasini o'z so'zlaringiz bilan 3–5 so'zda ifodalash, keyin uni sarlavhalar bilan solishtirish va ishlatilgan sarlavhalarni o'chirib borish.

Reading'ning boshqa savol turlari bo'yicha umumiy strategiyalar: [IELTS Reading strategiyalari](/blog/ielts-reading-strategiyalari).

## Nega Matching Headings qiyin

- Sarlavhalar paragraflardan ko'p — 2–3 tasi "ortiqcha" tuzoq.
- Sarlavhalar parafraz qilingan: matndagi so'zlar ularda deyarli takrorlanmaydi.
- Ko'p sarlavhalar mavzuga yaqin, faqat urg'u farq qiladi (*causes* va *effects*, *past* va *future*).
- Odatda u matndagi birinchi savollar to'plami bo'ladi, ya'ni vaqt bosimi boshlanishidan oldin vahima qilish oson.

## Asosiy g'oya va detalni ajrating

Paragrafda odatda bitta **asosiy g'oya** va uni qo'llab-quvvatlovchi **detallar** (misollar, raqamlar, ismlar) bo'ladi. Tuzoq sarlavhalar ko'pincha detalga mos keladi.

Namunaviy paragraf (o'zimiz tuzgan):

> *For centuries, farmers in dry regions have relied on underground channels to move water from mountain springs to their fields. In parts of Central Asia, some of these channels are still in use today. Yet their survival is under threat: as younger people move to cities, fewer families remain who know how to clean and repair them.*

Variantlar:

- i. *An ancient technology still used in Central Asia*
- ii. *Why traditional water channels may disappear*
- iii. *How mountain springs are formed*

**Tahlil:** paragraf qadimgi kanallar haqida boshlanadi (i variantga mos detal), lekin **asosiy g'oya** — *Yet their survival is under threat* burilishidan keyin keladi: bu texnologiya yo'qolib ketish xavfi ostida. Shuning uchun to'g'ri javob — **ii**. Variant iii esa faqat bitta so'zga (*springs*) asoslangan tuzoq.

*Yet, however, but, although* kabi burilish so'zlari ko'pincha asosiy g'oya qayerda ekanini ko'rsatadi.

## Bosqichma-bosqich usul

### 1. Sarlavhalarni qisqa ko'zdan kechiring (1 daqiqa)

Har bir sarlavhaning **asosiy so'zining** tagiga chizing: *causes, solution, comparison, history, criticism, future, benefits*. Bu so'zlar sarlavhaning "turi"ni ko'rsatadi. Sarlavhalarni yodlab olishga urinmang.

### 2. Paragrafni o'qing

- Birinchi va ikkinchi gapni diqqat bilan o'qing.
- Qolgan qismini tez ko'zdan kechiring, burilish so'zlari va oxirgi gapga e'tibor bering.
- Raqam va ismlarga berilib ketmang — ular odatda detal.

### 3. Asosiy g'oyani o'z so'zlaringiz bilan yozing

Paragraf yoniga 3–5 so'z yozing, masalan: *channels → threatened*. Bu eng muhim qadam: sarlavhalarni o'qishdan **oldin** paragrafni o'zingiz "nomlaysiz".

### 4. Sarlavhalar bilan solishtiring

O'z nomingizga ma'no jihatdan eng yaqin sarlavhani tanlang. Ikki variant mos kelsa, ikkalasini ham yozing va davom eting.

### 5. Ishlatilgan sarlavhani o'chiring

Har bir aniq javobdan keyin sarlavhani chizib qo'ying — tanlov kamaygani sari qolgan paragraflar osonlashadi.

### 6. Avval oson paragraflar

Ba'zi paragraflar juda aniq (masalan, faqat raqamlar va natijalar). Ularni birinchi yeching. Qiyin paragraflarga oxirida qaytasiz, chunki ungacha variantlar kamayib bo'ladi.

## Sarlavha turlari bo'yicha signal so'zlar

| Sarlavha turi | Paragrafda nimani qidirish kerak |
|---|---|
| Sabab (*reasons, causes, why*) | *because, due to, as a result of, led to* |
| Natija / ta'sir (*effects, impact, consequences*) | *therefore, resulted in, consequently* |
| Muammo (*problem, challenge, threat*) | *difficulty, risk, however, unfortunately* |
| Yechim (*solution, response, approach*) | *one way, measures, introduced, proposed* |
| Taqqoslash (*comparison, differences*) | *whereas, unlike, compared with, similarly* |
| Tarix (*origins, early, development*) | sanalar, *originally, in the past, first* |
| Kelajak (*prospects, future, predictions*) | *will, is likely to, in the coming years* |
| Tanqid / bahs (*criticism, controversy, debate*) | *critics, argue, opponents, disputed* |

## To'liq misol: 3 paragraf, 5 sarlavha

Quyidagi qisqa matn o'quv maqsadida tuzilgan:

> **A** — *The idea of a four-day working week is not new. As early as the 1970s, several companies experimented with longer days in exchange for an extra day off, although most trials were abandoned within a few years.*
>
> **B** — *Supporters of the shorter week point to recent trials in which employees reported lower stress and took fewer sick days. Some firms also found that productivity remained the same or even improved.*
>
> **C** — *Nevertheless, the model is not suitable for every sector. Hospitals, schools and shops need staff to be available throughout the week, and for them a shorter week could simply mean hiring more people.*

**Sarlavhalar:**

- i. *Why some industries may struggle to adopt the change*
- ii. *Evidence of the benefits for workers and employers*
- iii. *How governments regulate working hours*
- iv. *Early attempts at a shorter week*
- v. *The reasons employees prefer working from home*

**Tahlil:**

- **A → iv.** Asosiy g'oya: *1970-yillardagi dastlabki tajribalar*. Signal: *as early as the 1970s*, *trials were abandoned*.
- **B → ii.** Asosiy g'oya: *qisqa haftaning foydalari dalillari* — xodimlar uchun (stress kamaygan) va ish beruvchilar uchun (unumdorlik).
- **C → i.** *Nevertheless* burilishi — muammo: hamma soha uchun mos emas.
- **iii** va **v** — tuzoq. *Working hours* va *employees* so'zlari matnga yaqin, lekin hukumat tartibga solishi yoki uydan ishlash haqida paragraf yo'q.

## Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

- **So'z ovlash:** paragrafda va sarlavhada bir xil so'z bor — darhol tanlash. Bir xil so'z ko'pincha tuzoq.
- **Faqat birinchi gapga tayanish:** paragraf misol bilan boshlansa, asosiy g'oya keyinroq keladi.
- **Bitta paragrafda 4–5 daqiqa qolib ketish.** Ikki variant yozib, davom eting.
- **Sarlavhani juda tor tushunish:** *Benefits for workers and employers* — paragrafda ikkala tomon ham bo'lishi kerak.

## Vaqt rejasi

Matching Headings odatda 5–7 savoldan iborat. Taxminiy mo'ljal: **har bir paragrafga 1–1.5 daqiqa**, jami 8–10 daqiqa. Bu vaqtda siz butun matnning tuzilmasini ham bilib olasiz, shuning uchun keyingi savollar tezroq yechiladi.

## Mashq usuli

1. **Sarlavhasiz mashq:** istalgan ilmiy-ommabop maqolani oling va har bir paragrafga o'zingiz 5 so'zli sarlavha yozing. Bu ko'nikma imtihonda asosiy g'oyani tez topishga o'rgatadi.
2. **Tuzoqlarni tahlil qiling:** har bir xatodan keyin tanlagan sarlavhangiz paragrafning qaysi **detaliga** mos kelganini yozing.
3. **Sinonimlar daftari:** sarlavhalardagi so'zlar va matndagi ekvivalentlarini yozib boring (*drawbacks = disadvantages = downsides*). Bu True/False/Not Given'da ham yordam beradi — [TFNG va YNNG farqi](/blog/true-false-not-given-yes-no-not-given-farqi).

## Natija va keyingi qadam

Matching Headings — ko'pincha "hammasi yoki hech narsa" savol turi: asosiy g'oyani topishni o'rganganlar deyarli barcha savolni to'g'ri yechadi. Bu esa Reading'da 5–7 ta savol, ya'ni 1 band atrofida farq demakdir ([ball jadvallari](/blog/ielts-band-score-qanday-hisoblanadi)).

Strategiyani haqiqiy test sharoitida sinab ko'rish uchun [Mynine'da bepul onlayn mock test](/ielts-mock-test) topshiring va Reading natijangizni darhol bilib oling.

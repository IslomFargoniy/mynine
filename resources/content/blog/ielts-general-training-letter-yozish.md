**IELTS General Training Writing Task 1** da sizga hayotiy vaziyat beriladi va unga **kamida 150 so'zli xat** yozasiz. Xat formal (notanish odam yoki tashkilotga), semi-formal (tanish, lekin rasmiy munosabatdagi odamga) yoki informal (do'stga) bo'ladi. Yuqori ball uchun uchta narsa hal qiluvchi: **topshiriqdagi uchta bandning hammasiga javob berish**, **to'g'ri uslubni tanlash** va **aniq maqsad bilan boshlash**.

Academic va General Training farqi haqida batafsil: [IELTS Academic va General Training farqi](/blog/ielts-academic-va-general-farqi).

## Topshiriq qanday ko'rinadi

Namunaviy topshiriq (o'zimiz tuzgan):

> *You recently bought a coffee machine online, but it stopped working after one week.*
> *Write a letter to the company. In your letter:*
> - *give details of the purchase*
> - *describe the problem*
> - *say what you would like the company to do.*
>
> *Write at least 150 words. You do NOT need to write any addresses. Begin your letter as follows: Dear Sir or Madam,*

Uchta yo'naltiruvchi band — bu sizning reja. Ularning har biri uchun bitta paragraf yozasiz.

## Xat turini qanday aniqlash

Kimga yozayotganingizga qarang:

| Kimga | Tur | Murojaat | Yakun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kompaniya, bo'lim boshlig'i, notanish odam | Formal | *Dear Sir or Madam,* / *Dear Mr Aliyev,* | *Yours faithfully,* / *Yours sincerely,* + ism-familiya |
| Qo'shni, uy egasi, o'qituvchi, hamkasb | Semi-formal | *Dear Mrs Brown,* / *Dear Anvar,* | *Kind regards,* / *Best regards,* + ism |
| Do'st, qarindosh | Informal | *Dear Sam,* / *Hi Sam,* | *Best wishes,* / *Take care,* + ism |

Agar topshiriqda *Begin your letter as follows: Dear ...* deyilgan bo'lsa, shu murojaatni aynan ishlating.

### Uslubdagi farq

- **Formal:** qisqartmalarsiz (*I am writing*, *I would appreciate*), xushmuomala va aniq. So'zlashuv iboralari yo'q.
- **Semi-formal:** xushmuomala, lekin biroz iliqroq. Qisqartmalar kam.
- **Informal:** qisqartmalar (*I'm, can't*), savollar, hazil va shaxsiy ohang mumkin. Lekin bu "jargon"ga ruxsat degani emas.

Eng ko'p uchraydigan xato — do'stga juda rasmiy (*I am writing to inform you that...*) yoki kompaniyaga juda erkin (*Hey guys, your machine is rubbish*) yozish. Bu Task Achievement'dagi "tone" (ohang) talabiga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ta'sir qiladi.

## Universal tuzilma

1. **Murojaat** — *Dear ...,*
2. **Kirish (1–2 gap)** — nima uchun yozayotganingiz. Formal xatda birinchi gapdayoq maqsadni ayting.
3. **1-paragraf** — birinchi bandga javob.
4. **2-paragraf** — ikkinchi bandga javob.
5. **3-paragraf** — uchinchi bandga javob.
6. **Yakunlovchi gap** — javob kutayotganingiz, minnatdorchilik yoki uchrashuv taklifi.
7. **Yakun va ism.**

Har bir bandga taxminan bir xil hajm ajrating. Bitta bandni ikki gap bilan "o'tkazib yuborish" — ball yo'qotishning eng oson yo'li.

## Namunaviy xat 1: formal (shikoyat)

Yuqoridagi kofe mashinasi topshirig'i bo'yicha:

> Dear Sir or Madam,
>
> I am writing to complain about a coffee machine I purchased from your website and to request a replacement.
>
> I ordered the machine, model CM-200, on 3 March, and it was delivered two days later. The order number is 48213, and I paid by bank card. I chose this particular model because your website described it as reliable and suitable for daily use.
>
> Unfortunately, after only one week the machine stopped working. At first, it began making a loud noise whenever I switched it on, and then it stopped heating the water altogether. I have carefully followed the instructions in the manual, including cleaning the filter, but the problem has not been resolved.
>
> I would therefore be grateful if you could arrange for the faulty machine to be collected and replaced with a new one as soon as possible. If a replacement is not available, I would like a full refund. I can provide photographs of the machine and a copy of the receipt if necessary.
>
> I look forward to hearing from you.
>
> Yours faithfully,\
> Aziz Rahimov

(taxminan 185 so'z)

## Namunaviy xat 2: semi-formal (qo'shniga)

**Topshiriq:** *You are planning a small party at your flat next weekend. Write a letter to your neighbour. In your letter: explain why you are having the party, give details of the arrangements, and say what you will do to reduce any inconvenience.*

> Dear Mrs Williams,
>
> I hope you are keeping well. I am writing to let you know that I am planning a small get-together at my flat next Saturday evening.
>
> The reason for the party is that I have just finished my university degree, and some friends and family members would like to celebrate with me. It will be a fairly modest event, as I am expecting around fifteen guests.
>
> The party will start at about 6 pm, and I have told everyone that it will finish by 10:30 pm. We are planning to have dinner and play some board games, so it should not be a particularly loud evening.
>
> Nevertheless, I realise that it may cause some disturbance. I will make sure the music is kept low and ask guests not to gather on the staircase. I have also arranged for people to park in the street rather than in our shared car park. Of course, if there is any problem on the night, please feel free to knock on my door. You would also be very welcome to join us for a cup of tea.
>
> Kind regards,\
> Malika

(taxminan 195 so'z)

## Namunaviy xat 3: informal (do'stga)

**Topshiriq:** *A friend from another country is going to visit your city for the first time. Write a letter to your friend. In your letter: say how you feel about the visit, suggest some places to visit, and give advice about what to bring.*

> Dear Tom,
>
> I was so excited to get your message! I can't believe you're finally coming to Samarkand — we've been talking about this for years.
>
> You'll be here for four days, so we've got plenty of time. On the first day, I'd definitely take you to Registan Square early in the morning, before it gets crowded; the light at that time is amazing for photos. After that, we could wander through Siyob Bazaar and try some fresh bread and dried fruit. If you're up for a short trip, there are some lovely villages in the mountains about an hour away, where we could have lunch with a view.
>
> As for what to pack, the weather in spring can be quite unpredictable, so bring a light jacket as well as T-shirts. Comfortable shoes are a must, because we'll be walking a lot. Oh, and bring a scarf if you want to visit any mosques.
>
> Let me know your flight details and I'll pick you up from the airport.
>
> Can't wait to see you!
>
> Best wishes,\
> Jasur

(taxminan 190 so'z)

## Har bir tur uchun foydali iboralar

**Maqsadni bildirish (kirish):**

- Formal: *I am writing to enquire about / to complain about / to apply for...*
- Semi-formal: *I am writing to let you know that...*
- Informal: *Thanks for your letter — it was great to hear from you!*

**Iltimos qilish:**

- Formal: *I would be grateful if you could...* / *Could you please let me know...*
- Informal: *Could you do me a favour and...?*

**Uzr so'rash:**

- Formal: *I apologise for any inconvenience this may cause.*
- Informal: *Sorry I haven't been in touch for so long.*

**Yakunlovchi gap:**

- Formal: *I look forward to hearing from you.* / *Thank you for your attention to this matter.*
- Informal: *Write back soon!* / *Can't wait to see you.*

## Baholash: nimaga e'tibor beriladi

Task 1 ham to'rt mezon bo'yicha baholanadi: Task Achievement, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource va Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Xat uchun Task Achievement'da quyidagilar muhim:

- uchta bandning hammasi yoritilganmi va kengaytirilganmi;
- xat maqsadi aniqmi;
- ohang (formal yoki informal) qabul qiluvchiga mosmi va butun xat davomida izchilmi.

Writing bahosi qanday hisoblanishi (Task 2 ning ikki baravar og'irligi) haqida: [IELTS band score qanday hisoblanadi](/blog/ielts-band-score-qanday-hisoblanadi).

## Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

- **Bandlardan birini tashlab ketish** yoki unga bir gap bilan javob berish.
- **Ohangni aralashtirish:** formal xat boshida *Dear Sir or Madam*, oxirida *Cheers!*
- **Manzil va sana yozish** — kerak emas va vaqtni oladi.
- **Detallar yo'qligi:** "there is a problem" — qanday muammo? qachondan beri? nima qildingiz?
- **Yodlangan uzun kirishlar:** *I hope this letter finds you in the best of health and spirits...* — informal xatda bir qisqa gap yetarli.
- **20 daqiqadan ko'p vaqt sarflash** — Task 2 uchun 40 daqiqa kerak.

## 20 daqiqalik reja

| Bosqich | Vaqt |
|---|---|
| Topshiriqni o'qish, xat turini aniqlash, har bandga 2–3 ta g'oya yozish | 3 daqiqa |
| Yozish | 14 daqiqa |
| Tekshirish: uchta band, ohang, yakun iborasi, artikl va zamonlar | 3 daqiqa |

## Mashq qilish usuli

1. Har kuni bitta xat: bir kun formal, bir kun semi-formal, bir kun informal.
2. Yozib bo'lgach, har bir bandni rangli qalam bilan belgilang — uchala band ham ko'rinib turishi kerak.
3. Haftada bir marta Task 1 + Task 2 ni birga, 60 daqiqada yozing.

Writing'ni imtihon sharoitida yozib, uni to'rt mezon bo'yicha taxminiy baholatish uchun [Mynine'da bepul onlayn mock test](/ielts-mock-test) topshiring. Task 2 insho haqida: [IELTS Writing Task 2 qo'llanmasi](/blog/ielts-writing-task-2-qanday-yoziladi).

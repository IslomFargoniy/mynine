**True/False/Not Given** savollarida matndagi **faktlarni**, **Yes/No/Not Given** savollarida esa **muallifning fikrini** tekshirasiz. Yechish qoidasi ikkalasida bir xil: matn gapni tasdiqlasa — True/Yes, matn gapga **aniq zid** bo'lsa — False/No, matnda bu haqda **ma'lumot yo'q** bo'lsa — Not Given. Eng ko'p xato False va Not Given'ni aralashtirishdan kelib chiqadi, shuning uchun bu maqolaning asosiy qismi aynan shu farqqa bag'ishlangan.

Reading'ning boshqa savol turlari va vaqt taqsimoti haqida: [IELTS Reading strategiyalari](/blog/ielts-reading-strategiyalari).

## Uch javobning aniq ta'rifi

| Javob | TFNG (faktlar) | YNNG (muallif fikri) | Tekshiruv savoli |
|---|---|---|---|
| True / Yes | Matndagi ma'lumot gapni tasdiqlaydi | Muallif shu fikrda | Matnda gapning ma'nosi bormi? |
| False / No | Matndagi ma'lumot gapga zid | Muallif teskari fikrda | Matndan gapni **rad etuvchi** joyni ko'rsata olamanmi? |
| Not Given | Matnda ma'lumot yo'q yoki yetarli emas | Muallif bu haqda fikr bildirmagan | Na tasdiq, na rad — faqat jimlik |

## Oltin qoida: "rad etuvchi dalilni ko'rsating"

False javobini tanlashdan oldin o'zingizga savol bering: **"Matnning qaysi so'zi yoki gapi bu gapni noto'g'ri qiladi?"** Agar barmog'ingiz bilan aniq joyni ko'rsata olsangiz — False. Agar "matnda bunday deyilmagan, demak noto'g'ri" deb o'ylasangiz — bu Not Given.

"Aytilmagan" va "teskarisi aytilgan" — ikki xil narsa.

## 1-matn: TFNG uchun tahlilli misollar

Quyidagi matn va savollar o'quv maqsadida o'zimiz tuzgan:

> *The first public library in the town opened in 1912, funded largely by donations from local merchants. Initially, it lent books only to adult residents, but in 1935 a separate children's section was added. Today the library holds around 80,000 items, and its most popular service is the free English conversation club, which meets every Thursday.*

**1. The library was paid for mainly by local traders.**\
**Javob: True.** *Funded largely by donations from local merchants* — *funded* = *paid for*, *largely* = *mainly*, *merchants* = *traders*. Ma'no bir xil, faqat so'zlar sinonimlar bilan almashtirilgan.

**2. Children were allowed to borrow books from the library when it first opened.**\
**Javob: False.** *Initially, it lent books only to adult residents* — matnda aniq teskari ma'lumot bor. Rad etuvchi so'z: **only to adult**.

**3. The children's section was larger than the adult section.**\
**Javob: Not Given.** Bo'limlar hajmi haqida hech narsa aytilmagan. Mantiqan taxmin qilish mumkin emas.

**4. The library currently has more than 100,000 items.**\
**Javob: False.** *Around 80,000 items* — 100,000 dan ko'p degan gapga zid.

**5. The English conversation club is the library's most popular service.**\
**Javob: True.** Matnda aynan shunday deyilgan.

**6. The English conversation club charges a small fee.**\
**Javob: False.** *The free English conversation club* — "free" to'lovga zid. Ko'pchilik bu yerda "to'lov haqida gapirilmagan" deb Not Given tanlaydi, lekin *free* so'zi aniq rad etuvchi dalil.

**7. The conversation club was started by a former teacher.**\
**Javob: Not Given.** Klubni kim tashkil qilgani aytilmagan.

## 2-matn: YNNG uchun tahlilli misollar

> *Many commentators argue that remote work will soon replace the traditional office entirely. In my view, this prediction is exaggerated. While working from home clearly benefits employees with long commutes, it also makes it harder for new staff to learn informally from experienced colleagues. Companies that ignore this risk may save on rent but lose in the long term.*

**8. The writer believes that offices will disappear in the near future.**\
**Javob: No.** Muallif: *this prediction is exaggerated* — u bu fikrga qo'shilmaydi.

**9. The writer thinks remote work has advantages for some workers.**\
**Javob: Yes.** *Clearly benefits employees with long commutes.*

**10. The writer suggests that remote workers are less productive than office workers.**\
**Javob: Not Given.** Muallif yangi xodimlarning o'rganishi haqida gapiradi, lekin umumiy unumdorlikni solishtirmaydi. Bu — "mantiqan to'g'ri tuyuladigan" Not Given tuzog'i.

E'tibor bering: 8-savolda *many commentators argue* — bu boshqalarning fikri. YNNG savollarida faqat **muallifning** o'z pozitsiyasi hisoblanadi.

## Eng ko'p uchraydigan tuzoqlar

### 1. Kuchli so'zlar (qualifiers)

Savoldagi *all, always, never, only, most, some, usually* kabi so'zlar javobni butunlay o'zgartirishi mumkin.

| Matn | Savol | Javob |
|---|---|---|
| *Some* species migrate in winter. | *All* species migrate in winter. | False |
| The method is *often* used. | The method is *always* used. | False |
| The method is used. | The method is used *only* in Europe. | Not Given (agar joy aytilmagan bo'lsa) |

### 2. Taqqoslashlar

Savolda *more than, the first, the largest, earlier than* bo'lsa, matnda **ikkala tomonning ham** ma'lumoti bormi, tekshiring. Faqat bittasi tilga olingan bo'lsa — ko'pincha Not Given.

### 3. Shaxsiy bilim

Matnda *the Amazon river* deb yozilgan, savolda *The Amazon is the longest river in South America.* Siz buni bilasiz, lekin matnda aytilmagan bo'lsa — **Not Given**. Faqat matnga tayaning.

### 4. So'z mosligi, ma'no emas

Savoldagi so'zlar matnda aynan takrorlanishi ma'no mosligini kafolatlamaydi. Ko'pincha aynan takrorlangan so'zlar tuzoq bo'ladi, to'g'ri javob esa sinonimlar orqali beriladi (*merchants = traders*).

### 5. Sabab-natija

Matn: *Sales rose, and the company opened new stores.* Savol: *The company opened new stores because sales rose.* Matnda sabab bog'lanishi yo'q — **Not Given**.

## Bosqichma-bosqich yechish usuli

1. **Savoldagi kalit so'zlarning tagiga chizing** — ayniqsa ismlar, raqamlar, sanalar va kuchli so'zlar (*only, all, first*).
2. **Matnda tegishli joyni scanning bilan toping.** Javoblar savollar tartibida keladi.
3. **O'sha gapni va undan oldin-keyingi gapni diqqat bilan o'qing.**
4. **Ma'noni solishtiring, so'zlarni emas.**
5. **Qaror qiling:** tasdiq — True; aniq rad etuvchi dalil — False; ikkalasi ham yo'q — Not Given.
6. **2 daqiqadan ko'p o'ylamang.** Javob topilmasa, Not Given ehtimoli yuqori — belgilang va keyingisiga o'ting.

## Javoblar taqsimoti haqida afsona

"Har bir to'plamda True, False va Not Given teng bo'ladi" degan fikr bor. Bunga tayanmang: ba'zi to'plamlarda biror variant umuman uchramasligi yoki bir necha marta ketma-ket kelishi mumkin. Har bir savolni mustaqil yeching.

## Mashq usuli

- Har kuni bitta TFNG yoki YNNG to'plamini yeching.
- Har bir javob uchun matndagi **dalil gapni** yozib qo'ying. Not Given uchun esa "nimani qidirdim va nega topmadim" deb yozing.
- Xatolarni turlarga ajrating: kuchli so'z, taqqoslash, shaxsiy bilim, sabab-natija.
